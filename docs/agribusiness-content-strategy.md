# Agribusiness Content Strategy

Shields Farms is the initial content publisher. Noble Farms remains commerce-only and should not publish duplicate copies of Shields Farms articles.

## Editorial Mix

Use this as planning guidance, not a hard validation rule:

- 55% Nigeria/Africa practical guides and problem-solving content.
- 20% commercial buying guides and comparisons.
- 15% video companion content.
- 5% useful calculators and resources.
- 5% Shields Farms field notes and operations.

## Content Standards

Write for practical farmers, poultry operators, produce buyers, food businesses and small agribusiness teams. Use helpful answer summaries, key takeaways, descriptive headings, source citations, visible author details and reviewed/updated dates.

Avoid fake reviews, fabricated personal testing, unsupported ranking claims, copied merchant descriptions, hidden keyword blocks and thin affiliate pages. Structured data must match visible page content.

## Recommendation Basis

Every affiliate offer should use one of:

- `personally_tested`
- `editorial_research`
- `merchant_information`

A personally-tested claim needs an explicit methodology note. Editorial research should cite sources. Merchant information should be labelled clearly and should not be presented as independent testing.

## Topic Clusters

Initial clusters can cover poultry farming, egg production, crop production, farm tools, post-harvest handling, farm finance, food business supply, market insights, farm technology and Shields Farms operations.
