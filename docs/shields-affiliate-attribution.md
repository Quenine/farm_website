# Shields Farms affiliate attribution

The privacy preference UI synchronizes a first-party farm_consent_preferences cookie containing only analytics and marketing booleans plus a schema version. It contains no identity, email, cart, order or customer data.

/recommend/[slug] resolves and validates the active offer and partner on the server. The destination URL is retrieved only in that redirect route and never included in public article models. Navigation continues if optional logging fails. A click is recorded only when marketing consent is present.

Content-product redirects use analytics consent for optional click reporting and always preserve the essential farm_content_referral cookie. That cookie contains content/product identifiers, slugs and time only.

Affiliate networks remain the source of truth for merchant conversions and commission. Compare Shields click counts by offer, article and date with the network report. Differences may result from consent, blocking or network attribution windows. Do not interpret clicks as earnings.

To disable a compromised offer immediately, mark it inactive or move it to Trash. Public recommendations then become unavailable while the destination remains server-only.
